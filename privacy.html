Privacy Policy for Rotfall

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Rotfall is an Android mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and processed when you use the game.

Information collected by Rotfall

Rotfall does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Rotfall may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. These services may automatically collect certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, advertisement interactions, and diagnostic information.

This information may be used to provide, measure and improve advertising and to prevent fraud.

Third-Party Services

Rotfall may use third-party services for advertising, distribution, analytics, crash reporting, or other game functionality. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children

Rotfall does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where applicable, advertising and data processing will be configured in accordance with applicable age requirements and Google Play policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Rotfall does not currently maintain user accounts or a server-side database containing players' personal information.

Locally stored game data can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game. Information collected independently by third-party services is subject to those providers' retention and deletion policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Rotfall develops or its services change. The latest version will be made available on this page.
